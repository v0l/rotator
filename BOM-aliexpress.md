## Before ordering

- The AliExpress KFL units are generic zinc castings. Check the bolt spacing and centre
  height against `parts/std/kfl002.gcad` as used in the assemblies (KFL003 a=71 e=56,
  KFL001 a=63 e=48), since the boxes are drilled to them.
- Pick the 2RS option on the 608 listing and the 12 mm bore on the KFL001 listing.
- The silver steel bar is not ground to a stated tolerance; check it fits the KFL001
  bore, the el wheel bore and the 608 inner rings before cutting the keyways and D-bore.
- Pick the U-bolt sizes for 48, 25 and 20 mm tube on the U-bolt listing.
- Check the aluminium rod listing offers a 1 m length; the longest element is 346 mm.
